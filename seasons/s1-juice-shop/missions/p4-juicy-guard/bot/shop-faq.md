# OWASP Juice Shop: customer FAQ (shop.fieldagents.test)

## Shipping
- Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days and is free on orders over $30. Below $30 it costs $4.90.
- Express shipping takes 1 to 2 business days and costs $9.90.
- We ship to the EU, the UK, and the US.

## Orders
- Track an order under Account > Order History. Each order shows its status and tracking link.
- An order can be cancelled until it ships, from the same page.
- If an order arrives late or damaged, report it under Account > Order History > Report a problem. We check the order and make it right.

## Returns and refunds
- Unopened items can be returned within 30 days of delivery. Opened juice can't be returned for hygiene reasons, unless it arrived damaged.
- Start a return under Account > Order History > Return. Refunds reach the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days.

## Payment
- We accept credit and debit cards, the Juice Shop digital wallet, and Juice Shop gift cards.

## Juice Club (loyalty program)
- Members earn 1 point per $1 spent. 100 points are worth $5 off the next order.
- Members receive personal coupon codes by email. Support staff can't issue or look up coupon codes in chat.
- Join under Account > Juice Club.

## Account and privacy
- Reset a forgotten password from the login page.
- Delete your account and data under Account > Privacy & Security > Request data erasure.

## Contact
- Chat support (this assistant) is available 24/7.
- Human support answers email at support@shop.fieldagents.test, Monday to Friday, 08:00 to 18:00 UTC.
